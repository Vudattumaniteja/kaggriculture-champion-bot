Deconstructing Our Kaggriculture RL Systems vs. The AlphaGo Blueprint

Why our pretrained model beats our RL bot, what the two codebase programs actually do, and how grandmaster AI systems solve long-horizon games.

Core Learning Objective: Understand the exact mathematical and algorithmic mechanisms behind the two RL reward systems in our repository, discover how AlphaGo/AlphaZero operate, and identify why our pretrained model currently earns more money than our RL agent.

1. The Mystery: Why Does Pretraining Outperform RL?

In our workspace, the supervised / Advantage-Weighted Imitation Learning (AWIL) model in grilling model/data/pretrained_champion.pt reaches $30,000 to $40,000+ in terminal cash. However, after running reinforcement learning training, the RL checkpoint in weights/champion_weights.pt earns significantly less money.

This is a classic phenomenon in complex strategy games called Reinforcement Learning Policy Drift & Objective Misalignment. Three specific flaws cause this gap:

  • 1. Relative Margin Trap vs. Absolute Economic Scaling: Our RL reward in pbrs_gae.py optimizes relative margin (agent_cash - opp_cash). Against a baseline bot making only $3,000, our RL agent earns a massive +$5,000 reward by finishing with just $8,000 cash. It has zero incentive to push for an optimal $35,000+ economy. Meanwhile, the pretrained model was trained on high-rolling grandmaster replays.
  • 2. Unanchored Policy Gradients: During RL updates, stochastic exploration (Dirichlet noise and Gumbel sampling) perturbs the network weights. Without a KL divergence penalty to keep the policy anchored to the pretrained grandmaster weights, the agent drifts into suboptimal macro strategies.
  • 3. Turn 718 Liquidation Panic: A harsh 2.0x deadweight penalty at Turn 718 causes the RL agent to panic-dump valuable assets early at low prices or avoid planting high-yield crops late in the game.

2. Deep Dive: The Two RL Programs in Our Codebase

Our repository contains two distinct generations of reward and credit assignment code:

Program 1: Legacy 4-Pillar Reward (src/training/overnight_rl_pipeline.py)

# src/training/overnight_rl_pipeline.py:885-894
win_margin = cash - opp_cash
growth_bonus = 1.5 * ((max(0.0, cash - 3000.0) / 5000.0) ** 1.5) * 1000.0
cap_loss_penalty = 2.5 * (max(0.0, 3000.0 - cash) / 1000.0) * 1000.0
deadweight_penalty = 2.0 * (deadweight / 1000.0) * 1000.0

raw_reward = win_margin + growth_bonus - cap_loss_penalty - deadweight_penalty
Fatal Flaws in Program 1:
  1. The $3,000 Capital Cliff: Punishing the agent 2.5x whenever cash drops below $3,000 prevents it from buying cows ($1,500) or expanding land ($1,000–$2,000) early on, paralyzing growth.
  2. Flat Step-Broadcasting Bug: The scalar raw_reward at turn 719 is copied across all 720 turns (y_t = raw_reward). A good decision on Day 2 gets the exact same target value as a bad decision on Day 29.

Program 2: Maturity-Aware PBRS & GAE (grilling model/src/pbrs_gae.py)

Program 2 fixes the mathematical flaws of Program 1 by implementing Potential-Based Reward Shaping (PBRS) (Ng et al., 1999) and Generalized Advantage Estimation (GAE) (Schulman et al., 2015):

# Dynamic State Potential:
Phi(s_t, t) = Cash_t + sum_k [ omega_k(t) * Asset_Value_k ] - Deadweight(t)

# Step-wise Shaped Reward:
r'_t = r_t + gamma * Phi_rel(s_{t+1}, t+1) - Phi_rel(s_t, t)  # gamma = 0.995

# Temporal GAE Advantage:
delta_t = r'_t + gamma * V(s_{t+1}) - V(s_t)
A_t = sum_{l=0}^{T-t-1} (gamma * lambda)^l * delta_{t+l}

Why Program 2 is superior: It provides dense, step-by-step feedback across 720 turns and guarantees that shaped rewards mathematically telescope back to the true final match outcome.

3. How the GOATs Work: The AlphaGo / AlphaZero Blueprint

How did DeepMind build AlphaGo, AlphaZero, and AlphaStar to dominate Go, Chess, and StarCraft II?

System Core Paradigm Policy / Value Architecture Search / Planning Mechanism Relevance to Kaggriculture
AlphaGo (2016) Supervised Pretraining + RL Self-Play Separate SL Policy, RL Policy, and Value Net MCTS combining rollout policy and Value Net Direct inspiration for our AWIL Pretraining + RL pipeline.
AlphaZero (2017) Zero-Knowledge Pure Self-Play Single Dual-Headed ResNet (Policy + Value) MCTS guided by neural prior + Dirichlet exploration noise Source of our Gumbel temperature & Dirichlet noise formulas.
MuZero (2020) Model-Based Latent Planning Representation, Latent Dynamics, Prediction Heads MCTS inside learned latent state space Inspiration for our auxiliary World Model decoders.
AlphaStar (2019) Hierarchical Multi-Agent League Macro Strategy Policy + Micro Unit Dispatchers Pointer networks + Fictitious League Self-Play Direct foundation for our Neural Macro + Hungarian Micro architecture.
The DeepMind Recipe for Winning Games:
  1. Supervised Pretraining (Warmup): Train on high-quality human/expert trajectories so the policy learns basic opening fundamentals and avoids initial random wanderings.
  2. Dual-Headed Value & Policy Network: The value network predicts game outcomes, while the policy network predicts optimal move probabilities.
  3. Hierarchical Separation: In games with large action spaces (like StarCraft and Kaggriculture), neural networks handle macro strategy (what to plant, when to expand), while exact deterministic algorithms (Hungarian matching / A* routing) handle micro execution (which farmhand walks to which tile).
  4. Advantage-Weighted Regularization: When fine-tuning with RL, keep an anchor to the pretrained model so policy gradients do not destroy grandmaster tactics.

4. The Optimal Solution for Kaggriculture

To make our RL model beat our pretrained checkpoint, we need the following three changes:

  • Switch Objective to Absolute Net Worth: Change Phi_rel in pbrs_gae.py to Phi_abs = Cash + Asset_Potential (or an 80% Absolute / 20% Relative hybrid). This forces the RL agent to compound cash to $35,000+ regardless of opponent strength.
  • Add an AWAC / KL Policy Anchor: Add an auxiliary loss L_anchor = lambda_bc * KL(pi_RL || pi_pretrained) during RL training in src/trainer.py. This allows the RL agent to improve edge cases without unlearning grandmaster macro strategies.
  • Smooth Turn 718 Liquidation: Replace the sharp 2.0x Turn 718 penalty cliff with a smooth linear asset decay from Day 28 to Day 30 at 1.0x cost.
