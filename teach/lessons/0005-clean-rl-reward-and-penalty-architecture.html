Part 1: The Macro vs. Micro Reward Architecture

In a pure macro architecture, people often ask: "Do we give RL rewards to the micro solver, to the macro policy, or to both?"

The Separation of Concerns

  • The Macro Neural Policy (Trained with RL):
    Receives Potential-Based Step Rewards ($r'_t$) derived from total farm net worth $\Phi(s, t)$. Its job is long-term capital allocation (which crops to prioritize, when to hire farmhands, when to expand land).
  • The Micro Solver (Deterministic Math):
    Does NOT learn via RL gradients. Instead, it uses a Deterministic Priority Utility Function ($U_{\text{task}}$) to rank immediate chores: 
    Task Priorities:
1. Feed hungry cows/sheep  -> Utility = 1200.0  (Prevents zero milk production)
2. Water drying crops      -> Utility = 1180.0  (Prevents crop death)
3. Place animals in coop   -> Utility = 1100.0  (Unlocks daily yield)
4. Harvest mature crops    -> Utility = 850-890  (Fills shed with sellable produce)
5. Plant seeds             -> Utility = 800.0 + (Heatmap Priority from Macro AI)
6. Clear weeds             -> Utility = 350.0   (Frees up tile space)

Why this works: The Macro AI learns where and what to prioritize through RL rewards. The micro solver deterministically routes the workers using exact shortest paths.

Part 2: How to Reward (The 5 Value Engines)

To reward compounding wealth without modifying the true game objective, we use Potential-Based Reward Shaping (PBRS) (Ng et al., 1999).

The state potential $\Phi(s_t, t)$ represents the Total Enterprise Value (TEV) across 5 distinct asset engines:

The 5 Value Engines

  1. Engine 1: Liquid Cash ($C_t$):
    Dollar-for-dollar valuation ($\Phi_{\text{cash}} = 1.0 \times \text{Cash}$). Cash is the ultimate score at Turn 719.
  2. Engine 2: Capital Land Expansion ($\Phi_{\text{land}}$):
    When the AI buys the Northeast quadrant for $1,000, it gains 25 new plantable tiles. The investment potential amortizes across remaining game turns: $$\Phi_{\text{land}}(t) = \text{Cost} \times \max\left(0, \frac{720 - t}{720}\right)$$
  3. Engine 3: Biological Crop Maturation ($\Phi_{\text{crops}}$):
    A melon seed costs $80 and yields $1,500 after 12 days. Its potential scales as it grows: $$\Phi_{\text{crop}}(t) = \text{ExpectedRevenue} \times \text{clamp}\left(\frac{720 - t - 24}{T_{\text{mat}} - 24}, 0.0, 1.0\right)$$
  4. Engine 4: Livestock Annuity Stream ($\Phi_{\text{animals}}$):
    Cows and sheep produce continuous daily yields (Milk $160, Wool $200). Valued at the discounted Net Present Value (NPV) of future yields minus feed costs: $$\Phi_{\text{animal}}(t) = \sum_{\tau = t}^{\min(720, t + 240)} \gamma^{\tau - t} \times (\text{ProductPrice} - \text{FeedCost})$$
  5. Engine 5: Shed Inventory Buffer ($\Phi_{\text{shed}}$):
    Harvested items waiting in the shed are valued at spot market prices, with a smooth discount starting on Day 28 to force orderly market sale.
The 80/20 Hybrid Blend:
$$r'_t = 0.80 \times \left[ \gamma \Phi_{\text{self}}(t+1) - \Phi_{\text{self}}(t) \right] + 0.20 \times \left[ \Delta \Phi_{\text{self}} - \Delta \Phi_{\text{opp}} \right]$$ 80% drives massive wealth creation ($35k–$40k+), while 20% maintains tournament match awareness.

Part 3: How NOT to Reward (The 5 Deadly Traps)

The Deadly Trap What the Engineer Intended How the AI Hacks It (Catastrophic Failure)
1. Verb / Action Bonuses
+10 per plant		 Encourage active farming The Cobra Effect: AI buys cheap wheat seeds, plants them, digs them up, and re-plants them in an infinite loop until bankrupt.
2. Raw Cash Hoarding
Reward = Cash Only		 Focus on terminal score Zero Investment: Spending $1,000 on cows shows up as a -$1,000 loss. The AI hoards starting $3,000 and never builds an economy.
3. Uniform Broadcasting
r_t = FinalScore / 720		 Give reward at every step Credit Smearing: A brilliant investment on Day 3 gets the exact same score as a terrible blunder on Day 29. Learning freezes.
4. Pure Zero-Sum Margin
Reward = MyCash - OppCash		 Beat the opponent Low-Effort Satisficing: Against a weak bot making $3,000, our AI settles for $8,000 (margin +$5,000) and stops scaling production.
5. Premature Liquidation Bonus
+Bonus for selling		 Encourage selling goods Panic Dumping: AI dumps valuable wheat instead of saving 2 units to feed cows, causing cows to starve and milk yields to crash.

Part 4: How to Penalize (Action Masks vs. Valid Penalties)

1. What NEVER to Penalize (Use Action Masking Instead)

Never give negative reward penalties (e.g. -100 points) for:

  • Trying to buy land or seeds without enough money.
  • Trying to plant on an untilled tile or outside owned boundaries.
  • Trying to hire more than the legal number of farmhands.
Why penalties fail: When an exploratory AI gets hit with negative penalties, it learns that PASS gives 0.0 penalty. The policy collapses into Policy Paralysis (Freezing).
The Solution (Pre-Softmax Action Masking):
Set illegal logits to $-\infty$ (-1e9) inside the neural network before sampling. Illegal moves become mathematically impossible to choose, giving zero penalty gradient variance.

2. What SHOULD be Penalized (Deadweight & Opportunity Cost)

Penalties must be structured as Opportunity Cost Decays inside the potential function $\Phi(s, t)$:

  • Stranded Seeds on Day 29: If seeds are bought too late to mature before Turn 720, their potential drops to $0.0$, generating an automatic natural negative step delta: $$r'_t = \gamma \Phi(t+1) - \Phi(t) < 0$$
  • Shed Overflow Spoilage: Items exceeding the 100-item shed capacity at midnight are destroyed, naturally dropping $\Phi_{\text{shed}}$.
  • Starved Animals: If an animal is not fed, it produces 0 milk/wool tomorrow, reducing expected NPV potential.

Part 5: How to "Grill" & Stress-Test Your Reward Program

Before running 500,000 training steps, you must Grill (Stress-Test) your reward function using unit tests.

The 4 Grilling Stress-Tests

  1. Test 1: The Telescoping Sum Invariance Test:
    Run a random 720-step game. Verify that: $$\sum_{t=0}^{719} \gamma^t r'_t \approx \gamma^{720} \text{Cash}_{720} - \text{Cash}_0$$ If the difference is greater than $0.01$, your reward function has a leak and will cause reward hacking!
  2. Test 2: The Loop-Farming Test (Zero Reward for Circular Actions):
    Simulate an agent that plants a seed and immediately digs it up. Verify that the net reward across the 2-step cycle is negative or zero ($\le 0.0$).
  3. Test 3: The Passive Bot Baseline Test:
    Run an agent that executes PASS for all 720 turns. Verify that its cumulative shaped reward is exactly $0.0$ (no free points for existing).
  4. Test 4: The Monotonic Investment Test:
    When cash is converted into high-ROI melon seeds on Day 2, verify that $\Phi(s_{t+1}) \ge \Phi(s_t)$ (investing in good assets does not produce a fake negative penalty).

Part 6: How to Build the RL Environment & Gym Wrapper

Here is the clean, modular Python environment wrapper structure that integrates our Pure Macro Policy, Hungarian Micro Solver, and PBRS Reward Engine: 

class KaggricultureMacroEnv:
    """
    Vectorized Gym-Style Environment for Pure Macro Reinforcement Learning.
    Translates high-level Macro actions into Hungarian micro execution and PBRS rewards.
    """
    def __init__(self, opponent_pool, reward_engine):
        self.reward_engine = reward_engine
        self.opponent_pool = opponent_pool

    def step(self, macro_actions: Dict[str, np.ndarray]):
        # 1. Micro Solver executes physical chores based on macro heatmaps
        farmer_act, hands_acts = solve_micro_actions(
            obs=self.current_obs,
            crop_heatmaps=macro_actions["crop_heatmaps"],
            livestock_quotas=macro_actions["livestock_quotas"]
        )
        
        # 2. Market Guardrails execute trades
        market_orders = apply_market_guardrails(
            market_fractions=macro_actions["market_fractions"],
            obs=self.current_obs, ...
        )

        # 3. Step the physical Kaggle simulator
        next_obs, raw_reward, done, info = self.kaggle_env.step({
            "farmer": farmer_act, "hands": hands_acts, "market": market_orders
        })

        # 4. Compute PBRS Shaped Step Reward (80% Wealth / 20% Margin)
        shaped_reward, metrics = self.reward_engine.compute_step_reward(
            s_t=self.current_obs,
            s_next=next_obs,
            player=0
        )
        
        self.current_obs = next_obs
        return next_obs, shaped_reward, done, metrics

Summary of the Master Reward Program

  1. Reward State Wealth ($\Phi$): Reward the 5 engines of value (Cash, Land ROI, Growing Crops, Livestock NPV, Inventory).
  2. Never Reward Action Verbs: Avoid the Cobra Effect and circular farming loops.
  3. Mask, Don't Penalize: Gray out illegal moves with pre-softmax action masks to prevent policy paralysis.
  4. Grill Before Training: Run telescoping invariance and loop-farming unit tests before launching 500k training steps.
