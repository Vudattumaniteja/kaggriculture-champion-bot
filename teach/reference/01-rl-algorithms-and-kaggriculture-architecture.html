2. The 2-Tier Hierarchical Paradigm

In complex strategy games, attempting to output raw robot/hand movements directly from a neural network creates a combinatorial explosion ($O(K! \cdot 5^K)$ moves per step). We solve this using DeepMind's hierarchical separation: 

[Raw Observation State (10x10 grid, cash, shed, shop, market)]
                      │
                      ▼
       [Feature Encoder (src/encoder.py)]
                      │  ├── 24-channel spatial tensor: (24, 10, 10)
                      │  └── 72-dimensional scalar economic vector
                      ▼
     [High-Level Macro Policy (src/network.py)]
                      │  ├── Crop Heatmaps (5, 10, 10)
                      │  ├── Livestock Quotas (Goose, Cow, Sheep)
                      │  ├── Workforce Expansion (Hiring logits)
                      │  ├── Land Expansion Logit (NE: $1k, SW: $2k, SE: $4k)
                      │  ├── Seed Replenish Allocations
                      │  └── Continuous Market Liquidation Fractions
                      ▼
  [Low-Level Micro Chore Solver (src/micro_solver.py)]
                      │
                      ├── 1. Generates atomic tasks (Feed, Water, Pasture, Harvest, Plant)
                      ├── 2. Solves Bipartite Matching via Scipy Linear Sum Assignment (Hungarian)
                      └── 3. Emits collision-free step movements & shed transfers
                      ▼
  [Final Turn Output: farmer action, hands actions, market queue]

3. Reward Shaping Mathematics & Core Formulas

Potential-Based Reward Shaping (PBRS)

Given state potential $\Phi(s, t)$, the transformed step reward is:

r'_t = r_t + gamma * Phi(s_{t+1}, t+1) - Phi(s_t, t)

Policy Invariance Theorem (Ng et al., 1999): Any policy $\pi^*$ that maximizes $\mathbb{E}[\sum \gamma^t r'_t]$ is guaranteed to maximize $\mathbb{E}[\sum \gamma^t r_t]$.

Generalized Advantage Estimation (GAE)

Balances bias (low $\lambda$) and variance (high $\lambda$) across multi-step rollouts:

delta_t = r'_t + gamma * V(s_{t+1}) - V(s_t)
A_t^{GAE} = sum_{l=0}^{T - t - 1} (gamma * lambda)^l * delta_{t+l}

Standard Parameters: $\gamma = 0.995, \lambda = 0.95$.

Advantage-Weighted Policy Loss (AWAC / AWIL)

Updates policy weights using exponentiated advantage weights while keeping policy bounded:

w_t = clip(exp((A_t - mu_A) / (tau * sigma_A)), 0.2, 5.0)
L_policy = - sum_t w_t * log pi_theta(a_t | s_t) + lambda_kl * D_KL(pi_theta || pi_pretrained)
